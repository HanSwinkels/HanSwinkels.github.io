Who we are

Arras is a darts scoring and training app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: almost everything stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Arras is stored locally in the app's own database on your device:

  • Player profiles (a display name and the preferences you set — Arras has no user accounts)
  • Match history, scores and statistics
  • Training sessions, drills, routines, weekly plans and your training profile (level, goals, focus areas)

This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync (below). We have no server and no access to it.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you use sync between your devices, Arras stores your data in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), tied to your own Apple ID. Only your devices can read it. We cannot access, view or recover it, and Apple's privacy policy governs the iCloud service itself.

Purchases

The training features are offered as an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never receive your name, address or payment details.

To validate purchases and unlock the features you paid for, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-infrastructure service acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app-generated identifier and your App Store receipt data (product, purchase and expiry dates). This identifier is not linked to your name, profile or any other data in the app.

You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your App Store account settings.

Notifications

Training reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No profiling across apps or websites

Deleting your data

You can delete individual profiles, matches and training data inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes the entire local database. If you used iCloud sync, you can additionally remove Arras data from iCloud in your device's iCloud storage settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. Because your data is stored on your own device and in your own iCloud rather than on our servers, you exercise these rights directly through the app and your device settings. For anything relating to purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will pass the request on.
